This policy describes what data Lumi collects when it operates in a Discord server, why it collects it, how long it is kept, and how you can request deletion. Lumi is operated by `@Naiyoko` ("we"). "API Data" below means data Lumi receives from Discord's API while providing its features.

## What Do We Store?

- User ID - Powers a user XP and level system.
- Guild ID - Ties each guild to its server-specific configuration.
- Invite information - For every partnership done, Nova must validate that the given invite link is actually valid, and get information to power variables (such as server name, server icon, among others) from the Discord API. To avoid API requests where possible, we cache this information temporarily. This information is automatically deleted after a set period.

## How Long Is My Data Stored?

Data is only stored as long as necessary to provide functionality, comply with our legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

## Information Processed But Not Stored
To provide functionality, we process some data that is never stored. This includes, but is not limited to:
- Message contents - Used only for checking if the message is a partnership or executing a ping command.
- Member list. Nova's `/managers list` command fetches a server's member list through Discord's Server Members Intent in order to show which members hold Nova's configured manager role(s).
- Member Join/Leave - used for the welcome and leave message functionality. *Note: This functionality is not yet implemented in the Beta.*

## Our Data Protection Policies
Any and all Discord API Data stored on our systems, including items mentioned under "**What Do We Store?**", are encrypted at rest. Beyond this, we take additional measures to ensure that any and all backups of our systems are also encrypted.

## Requesting Deletion
You can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us at `**luminousmanagement.help@gmail.com**` & sending a direct message to `@Naiyoko` via Discord, notifying us of your request. We may ask you to verify your identity depending on the data you are requesting, or the data you are requesting deletion of. Failing verification of your identity, we reserve the right to refuse data deletion or a copy of your data.

## Data Sharing Policies
No data we collect is ever sold or shared with any third parties. Data is used solely to provide Nova's features, as described above. We may share data when we are legally required to do so, such as by a court order or other valid legal process.
